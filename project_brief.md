# My agent: greenhouse-assistant
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant lovers care for their indoor plants and discover greenhouse species with a catalog of plants and care guides.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user's plant collection, watering history, sunlight conditions, and experience level
- Tools: Look up plant care instructions, check greenhouse inventory, log watering schedules
- Catalog/UI: Greenhouse plant inventory (name, species, price, care_level, sunlight, in_stock) rendered as cards and tables
- Image gen: Generates visual representations of thriving plants and custom green arrangements
- Sandbox: Calculates watering intervals and fertilizer dilution ratios

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for calculations, Perenual public plant API, Cloud Trace
